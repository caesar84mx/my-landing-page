01 · Definition

“Full-stack” means backend plus mobile clients.

This service is specifically for mobile products. The stack runs from server-side data, APIs, authentication, notifications, and integrations through Android and iOS applications, platform behavior, release automation, and store launch.

Web front-end development is not the offer. When a product needs a website or a specialist infrastructure layer, I coordinate that boundary without presenting it as my primary discipline.

02 · Scope

A complete technical path for the mobile product.

Product and architecture

I turn the business goal into a delivery plan, define system boundaries, choose native or cross-platform technology, and make the risks visible before implementation expands.

Backend and integrations

The server-side scope can include APIs, data models, authentication, billing, notification delivery, messaging providers, analytics boundaries, and the operational behavior the apps depend on.

Android and iOS applications

I build native Android and iOS experiences or use Kotlin Multiplatform when shared logic creates a real delivery advantage. Platform-specific behavior remains a first-class part of the architecture.

Release and handover

Delivery includes CI/CD, signing, store preparation, review support, release stability, documentation, and a practical handover.

03 · Fit

Best when the product needs ownership between the layers.

  • A new mobile product needs architecture, backend, Android, and iOS delivery.
  • The current apps depend on backend behavior that no single team owns end to end.
  • A cross-platform decision needs to account for platform APIs, release risk, and future maintenance.
  • The product needs a senior technical owner without building a complete permanent team first.
