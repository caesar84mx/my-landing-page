Mobile product consulting for difficult decisions.

A focused senior review of the product, architecture, platform constraints, and delivery plan—before uncertainty becomes an expensive implementation commitment.

Input
Goal · Codebase · Constraints · Team
Review
Architecture · Risk · Delivery path
Output
Findings · Decisions · Prioritized plan
Format
Focused audit or ongoing advisory
01 · When

Bring in a consultant before the decision hardens.

  • A new product needs an architecture and native-versus-cross-platform decision.
  • A legacy app makes routine delivery slower and riskier every quarter.
  • Performance, media, BLE, camera, maps, background execution, or store policy blocks the team.
  • Backend and mobile teams disagree on boundaries, ownership, or the realistic delivery sequence.
02 · Review

Technical analysis tied to a business outcome.

The engagement starts with the goal and constraints, then examines the architecture, current implementation, team capability, platform dependencies, data and backend boundaries, release process, and the assumptions carrying the most risk.

The result is not a generic best-practices report. Recommendations are prioritized by impact, cost, dependency, and the order in which the team can safely act.

  • Architecture
  • Native vs cross-platform
  • Performance
  • Legacy modernization
  • Backend boundaries
  • Platform APIs
  • Release risk
  • Team ownership
03 · Deliverable

A decision record and an executable next step.

Depending on the problem, the deliverable can include architecture findings, risk ranking, technology recommendations, target boundaries, migration stages, validation criteria, and a delivery roadmap sized for the actual team.

Risksranked by impact and likelihood
Decisionswith explicit trade-offs and constraints
Planprioritized into verifiable delivery stages
