01 · Symptoms

Modernization starts where delivery repeatedly breaks down.

  • Small features require changes across tightly coupled modules.
  • Performance problems are visible but their ownership is unclear.
  • Legacy framework or platform decisions block current Android and iOS capabilities.
  • Media, Bluetooth, camera, maps, or background execution fail in production edge cases.
  • Release risk prevents the team from replacing the architecture all at once.
02 · Approach

Replace risk in slices, not in one heroic rewrite.

I begin by identifying the product paths, architectural boundaries, and platform integrations with the highest cost of change. The target architecture is then expressed as migration stages that preserve working releases and produce evidence early.

Where the hard part is platform-specific, I work directly at that layer: Android media, BLE, Health Connect, camera, lifecycle, background execution, or the boundary between shared and native code.
