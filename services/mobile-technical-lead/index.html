01 · Fit

When the team can build but lacks one technical direction.

  • Android, iOS, backend, and product decisions are being made in isolation.
  • Senior reviews arrive too late to change the architecture safely.
  • A growing team needs standards, mentoring, and clearer ownership.
  • A migration or release program needs consistent technical judgment across several months.
02 · Role

Leadership stays close to the implementation.

I work with the team on architecture, scope, code and design reviews, release readiness, platform risks, and the decisions that cross mobile and backend boundaries.

The role can include mentoring engineers, refining requirements before implementation, reviewing difficult changes, establishing validation standards, and documenting the reasoning the team will need after the engagement.

The team keeps the capability, not just the decisions.
