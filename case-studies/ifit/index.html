Native Android modernization

Modernizing fitness experiences at product scale.

A multi-year contribution to iFIT’s move from Xamarin toward native Android across phones, tablets, and connected-equipment consoles.

Role
Senior Android Engineer · Contract
Period
July 2023 – January 2026
Scope
Mobile · Tablet · Equipment consoles
Path
Xamarin → Native Android
01 · Context

A mature platform with more than one Android surface.

The modernization covered consumer mobile experiences and connected fitness equipment. Changes had to work across different screen sizes, hardware integrations, media behavior, and release constraints while the existing product continued to ship.

02 · Contribution

Deep platform work inside a long-running migration.

Media reliability

I improved ExoPlayer behavior through playback fixes, closed-caption support, and custom video-scaling modes across mobile devices and equipment consoles.

Connected equipment

I built Bluetooth LE console integration and Health Connect synchronization end to end, and contributed Kotlin Multiplatform SDK components for live equipment telemetry.

Modernization delivery

Across more than 580 merged pull requests, I contributed implementation, review, release support, and technical-debt reduction throughout the move toward native Android.

  • Android
  • Kotlin
  • ExoPlayer
  • Bluetooth LE
  • Health Connect
  • Kotlin Multiplatform
  • Connected equipment
03 · Impact

Measurable improvement backed by sustained delivery.

The work contributed to substantial performance and technical-debt improvements across the mobile and equipment-console experiences.

580+merged pull requests
~50%performance improvement
~50%technical-debt reduction
